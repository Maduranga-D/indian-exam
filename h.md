# Bhatkhande Music Curriculum: Tabla & Pakhawaj (Percussion Syllabus)

---

## 1. Practical Syllabus

### 1.1 Tabla Practical

#### TABLA PRATHAMA (FIRST YEAR)
**Maximum Marks: 200**

1. **Ability to play in the following Talas:**
   * **Teentaal:** Two Qaidas with four Paltas and Tihai; Two Mukhras; Two Tukdas.
   * **Jhaptaal:** One Qaida with two Paltas and Tihai; One Mukhra; One Tukda.
2. **Non-Detailed Talas:** Ektal, Adachartal, Dhamar, Kaharwa, and Dadra.
   * Ability to play all the Thekas of the above Talas in *Thah* (Barabar) and *Dugun* Laya.
3. **Padhant:** Recitation showing *Tali-Khali* by hand gestures for all prescribed Talas, their Thekas, and compositions.
4. **Knowledge of Theka Variations:** Kaharwa and Dadra Taal.

> **Note:** There shall be no Theory paper in Prathama First Year (Tabla).

---

#### TABLA PRATHAMA (SECOND YEAR)
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Technique:** Proper technique of producing basic strokes and syllabus.
* **Prescribed Talas:**
  * **(A) Detailed:** Trital, Jhaptal, Ektal
  * **(B) Non-Detailed:** Tilwada, Adachartal, Jhoomra, Punjabi, Dhamar, Kaharwa, Dadra
* **Requirements:**
  * Two Kaydas with Paltas, Two Tukras, Two Relas, Two Mukhras, and Tihais in each Taal of Category 'A'.
  * Ability to play all the Thekas of Category 'B' Talas in *Thah*, *Dugun*, and *Chaugun* Laya.
  * Padhant of all prescribed Talas, their Thekas, and compositions with hand gestures (*Tali-Khali*).
  * Elementary knowledge of tuning the instrument.
  * Knowledge of Theka variations of Kaharwa & Dadra Taal.

---

#### TABLA MADHYAMA PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Trital:** Two advance Qaidas with five Paltas in each; Three advance Gats, two Mukhras, and Relas; Three Tukdas, simple Tihais, and playing Trital in *Adilaya*.
2. **Layakari Practice:** Ability to play the Thekas of the following Talas in *Barabar*, *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun*:
   * Adachartaal, Roopak, Sooltaal, Matta, Gajajhampa, Teora.
3. **Detailed Study (Adachartaal and Roopak):** Two Qaidas, Two Gats, Two Relas, Two Mukhdas, Two Tukdas, and advance Tihais in each Taal.
4. **Pakhawaj-oriented Talas (Chautaal and Sooltaal):** Four Tukdas, Two Parans (*Sada* and *Chakradar*), and advance Tihai in each Taal.
5. **Tuning:** Tuning of the instrument.
6. **Padhant:** Recitation of all prescribed Talas and Bols.

---

#### TABLA MADHYAMA PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Tritaal:** Three advance Qaidas with five Paltas each; Three advance Gats, Three Mukhras, Two Relas, Four Tukdas, Two Chakradar Tukdas, Two Sadi Parans, One Chakradar Paran, and Tihais (*Damdar* and *Bedam*).
2. **Layakari Practice:** Ability to play the Thekas of the following Talas in *Thah*, *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun*:
   * Farodast, Pancham Sawari (15 matras), Ashtamangal (11 matras), Dhamar, Basant (9 matras), Choutaal, and Rudra.
3. **Detailed Study (Pancham Sawari & Ektaal):** Two Qaidas with four Paltas each, Two Gats, Two Relas, Two Mukhdas, Two Tukdas, Two Sadi Parans, and advance Tihais in each Taal.
4. **Dhamar and Sooltaal:** Four Tukdas, Two Parans (*Sadi* and *Chakradar*), and advance Tihais in each Taal.
5. **Padhant:** Hand recitation showing *Tali-Khali* for all prescribed Talas, Thekas, and compositions.
6. **Tuning:** Perfect tuning of the instrument.

---

#### TABLA VISHARAD PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Trital:** Peshkar, Two advance Qaidas, Four advance Gats, Three Mukhdas, Four Tukdas, and Two Relas with eight Paltas in each.
2. **Talas Study:** Shikhar, Rudra, Yati Shikhar, and Chitra.
   * Play Thekas in *Barabar*, *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun* with simple Tukdas and Tihais.
3. **Other Talas:** Taal Farodast and Pashtu with simple Qaidas.
4. **Semi-Classical Accompaniment:** Use and execution of *Laggi* and *Ladi*.
5. **Padhant:**
   * Recitation of all learnt Talas and Bols.
   * Recitation of Thekas of prescribed Talas in *Adi* and *Kuadi* Layakaris.

---

#### TABLA VISHARAD PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Trital:** Advance Peshkars, Gats, Qaidas, Relas, Tukdas, Mukhdas, Parans, and advance compositions in *Khand*, *Tisra*, and *Misra Jatis*.
2. **Prescribed Talas:** Basant, Brahma, Laxmi, Vishnu, Ganesh, and Mani.
   * Performance ability in each Taal: Four Sada Tukdas, Two Chakradar Tukdas, Four Sada Parans, Two Kamali Chakradar Parans, Two Mukhdas, and Two Relas.
3. **Trital Layakari:** Ability to play the Theka of Trital in *Barabar*, *Dugun*, *Tigun*, *Chaugun*, and *Adi Laya*.
4. **Tuning:** Precision tuning of the instrument.
5. **Accompaniment Skills:** Playing Thekas of all prevalent Talas in *Vilambit Laya*, as well as accompanying string instruments, *Drut Khayal*, and *Tarana*.
6. **Padhant:** Mastery of reciting all learnt Talas and Bols in any given Laya.

---

#### TABLA NIPUN PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Gharana Study:** Comprehensive study of Trital, Jhaptal, Ektal, and Adachartaal featuring Delhi Peshkar, Qaidas, Paltas, Gats, Tukdas, and Relas (two each) in the style of **Delhi** and **Ajrala** Gharanas.
2. **Jati Variations:** One Gat in *Adilaya*, *Tisra*, and *Mishr Jati* in each Taal.
3. **Complex Compositions:**
   * Two Mukhdas of advance patterns in each Taal across various Layakaris.
   * Tihais (*Damdar* and *Bedam*) in all prescribed Talas.
   * One *Manjhedar Gat* in each Taal.
4. **Padhant:** Hand recitation of Thekas and compositions (Qaidas, Gats, Tukdas) for all prescribed Talas.

---

#### TABLA NIPUN PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Gharana Study:** Comprehensive study of Trital, Roopak, Sool, Sawari, and Dhamar featuring Delhi Peshkars, Qaidas, Paltas, Gats, Tukdas, and Relas (two each) in the style of **Ajrala** and **Farrukhabad** Gharanas.
2. **Complex Compositions:**
   * One Gat in *Kuwadi Laya*, *Khand*, and *Chatusra Jati* in each Taal.
   * Two Mukhdas of advance patterns in each Taal across different Layakaris.
   * Tihais (*Damdar* and *Bedam*) in all prescribed Talas.
   * One *Kamal-ki-Gat* and one *Farmaishi Gat* in each Taal.
3. **Padhant:** Recitation showing beat of hands for all prescribed Talas with Qaidas, Gats, Mukhdas, etc.

---

#### TABLA NIPUN PART - III
**Total Marks: 400**

> **Note:** There shall be no Theory paper in Nipun Part III. The practical exam is divided into two sections:

##### Part A: Stage Performance (M.M. 200)
* Practical solo demonstration and questionnaire on the following Talas:
  * **Trital, Rupak, Jhaptal, Ada-Chautaal, Basant, and Rudra.**
* *(Duration decided by the examiner)*

##### Part B: Viva-Voce (M.M. 200)
1. Comparative evaluation of *Gharana-Bajj*.
2. On-the-spot composition and formation of Tihais.
3. Explanation and practical examples of different types of Gats.
4. Explanation and practical examples of various Chakradars.
5. Demonstration of complex Layakaris (*Adi, Kuadi, Biyadi, Paungun*) across various Thekas and reciting them continuously within a single time cycle.
6. Theoretical and practical understanding of *Jatis*, *Yati*, and *Grah*.

---

### 1.2 Pakhawaj Practical

#### PAKHAWAJ PRATHAMA (FIRST YEAR)
**Maximum Marks: 200**

1. **Instrument Anatomy:** Description and study of the various structural parts of the Pakhawaj.
2. **Playing Technique:** Production of individual and joint strokes (*Varnas*) on the instrument.
3. **Detailed Talas:**
   * **Choutal:** Two easy Parans, Two Relas, Two Tihais.
   * **Sooltal:** Two easy Parans, Two Relas, Two Tihais.
4. **Non-Detailed Talas:** Ektaal, Adachartal, Dhamar, Teevra, Jhaptal, Kaharwa, and Dadra.
   * Ability to play Thekas by hand showing *Tali-Khali* in *Thah* and *Dugun* Laya.
5. **Layakari Practice:** Playing Thekas of Dhamar and Teevra in *Thah*, *Dugun*, and *Chaugun* Laya.

> **Note:** There shall be no Theory paper in Prathama Part I (Pakhawaj).

---

#### PAKHAWAJ PRATHAMA (SECOND YEAR)
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Basic Syllabus:** Techniques for producing basic strokes and syllabus.
* **Prescribed Talas:**
  * **(A) Detailed:** Choutal, Dhamar, Sooltal
  * **(B) Non-Detailed:** Teental, Teevra, Kaharwa, Dadra
* **Requirements:**
  * Two easy Parans and Two Relas in each Taal of Category 'A'.
  * Ability to play Thekas and variations of Category 'B' Talas.
  * Playing *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun* of all prescribed Talas.
  * Simple Tihais in all prescribed Talas.
  * Padhant of all prescribed Thekas and compositions.
  * Elementary knowledge of tuning the instrument.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ MADHYAMA PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study:** Sawari, Matt, Gajajhampa, and Chautal.
2. **Padhant:** Recitation in *Thah*, *Dugun*, and *Chaugun* of prescribed Talas.
3. **Execution of Bol Phrases:** Execution and usage of *Dhumkit* phrases across all prescribed Talas.
4. **Compositions (in each prescribed Taal):**
   * One Mohra
   * Two Sada Parans
   * One Chakradar Paran
   * One Rela
   * One Tisra Jati Paran
   * Simple Tihais (*Bedam* and *Damdar*)
5. **Padhant:** Hand recitation of all learnt Talas and Bols.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ MADHYAMA PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study:** Chautal, Dhamar, Basant (9 matras), and Matt (18 matras).
2. **Padhant:** Recitation in *Thah*, *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun*.
3. **Execution of Bol Phrases:** Application of *Dhumkit* phrases in all prescribed Talas.
4. **Compositions (in each prescribed Taal):**
   * Three Sada Parans
   * One Chakradar Paran
   * One Rela
   * One Tisra Jati Paran
   * Tihais (*Bedam* and *Damdar*)
   * One *Sath-Paran* in any one prescribed Taal.
5. **Padhant:** Recitation of all prescribed Talas and compositions.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ VISHARAD PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study with Advance Compositions:** Shikhar, Rudra, Yati-Shikhar, Dhamar, and Chautal.
2. **Padhant:** Recitation in *Dugun*, *Tigun*, and *Chaugun*.
3. **Compositions:**
   * One Chakradar Paran and Two Sada Parans in each Taal.
   * One *Adilaya-ki-Paran* in each Taal.
   * Tihais (*Bedam* and *Damdar*) in all prescribed Talas.
   * *Badhaiya-ki-Paran* in any two prescribed Talas.
   * *Sath-Paran* in any prescribed Taal.
   * One Rela in each prescribed Taal.
4. **Padhant:** Recitation of all prescribed Talas and compositions.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ VISHARAD PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study with Advance Compositions:** Basant, Brahma, Laxmi, Ganesh, Vishnu, and Mani.
2. **Compositions:**
   * One Chakradar Paran and Two Sada Parans in each Taal.
   * One *Adilaya-ki-Paran* in each Taal.
   * *Sath Parans* in all prescribed Talas.
   * One Mukhada or Mohara in each Taal.
   * Advance Tihais in all prescribed Talas.
3. **Theoretical Concepts:** Application of *Jatis*, *Yati*, and *Grah*.
4. **Tuning:** Perfect tuning of the Pakhawaj.
5. **Padhant:** Complete recitation mastery of all prescribed Talas and Bols.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ NIPUN PART - I
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study & Style:** Comprehensive study of Choutal, Dhamar, Teora, Sool, and Jhampa with all variations of Parans (e.g., *Sath Paran*, *Gat Paran*), with special emphasis on the **Panse Gharana**.
2. **Complex Layakari & Jatis:**
   * Parans in *Kuadi Laya*.
   * Parans in *Tisra* and *Misra Jatis*.
   * *Bedam* and *Damdar* Tihais in various Layakaris.
3. **Special Compositions:** Traditional *Ganesh Paran* in any Taal.
4. **Padhant:** Recitation of all learnt Bols.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ NIPUN PART - II
**Maximum Marks: 200**

* **Revision:** Revision of the previous syllabus.
1. **Detailed Study & Style:** Comprehensive study of Trital, Laxmi, Jhaptal, and Shikhar including all varieties of Parans (*Sath Parans*, *Gat Parans*, *Kavit Parans*, etc., both *Sada* and *Chakradar*). Special emphasis on the **Kudao Singh Gharana**.
2. **Complex Layakari:** Parans and Tukdas in *Biadi Laya* in prescribed Talas.
3. **Special Compositions:** Conventional *Top-ki-Paran* and *Gaj-paran* in any Taal.
4. **Padhant:** Full recitation of all learnt Bols.

---

#### PAKHAWAJ NIPUN PART - III
**Total Marks: 400**

> **Note:** There shall be no Theory paper in Nipun Part III. The practical exam is divided into two sections:

##### Part A: Stage Performance (M.M. 200)
* Practical demonstration and viva-voce examination on the following Talas:
  * **Chautaal, Dhamar, Ganesh, Rudra, and Lakshmi.**

##### Part B: Viva-Voce (M.M. 200)
1. Comparative study of *Gharana-Baj*.
2. Spontaneous formation of Tihais.
3. Detailed explanation with examples of different types of Gats.
4. Detailed explanation with examples of different types of Yatis.
5. Detailed explanation with examples of different types of Chakradars.
6. Practical demonstration of complex Layakaris (*Adi, Kuwadi, Biyadi, Paungun*) across various Thekas and speaking them in one continuous time cycle.

## 2. Theory Syllabus (Combined Tabla & Pakhawaj)

> **Rule:** Combined theory examination papers are conducted for Tabla and Pakhawaj/Mridang from **Prathama (2nd Year)** through **Nipun Part II**.

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ PRATHAMA (THEORY)
**Maximum Marks: 100**

1. General knowledge regarding the origin of Tabla and Mridang.
2. Structural description and study of various parts of Tabla and Mridang.
3. Technical production of syllabus (*Varnas*) individually and combined.
4. **Definitions of Musical Terms:**
   * Sangit, Tat Vadya, Avanaddha Vadya, Ghana Vadya, Bhari, Tali, Vibhag, Avartan, Theka, Vilambit, Madhya, Drut, Barabar, Dugun, Tigun, Chaugun.
5. **Definitions of Compositional Terms:**
   * Qaida, Gat, Mukhada, Rela, Tihai, Tukda, Paran, Mohara, Bol, Palte.
6. **Notation:** Method of writing notation for all prescribed Talas with their Thekas and Bols.
7. **Biographies:** Life sketches of legendary Tabla and Mridang exponents.

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ MADHYAMA PART - I (THEORY)
**Maximum Marks: 100**

* **Revision:** Review of previous theory syllabus.
1. **Pedagogy:** Merits and demerits of Tabla and Pakhawaj players.
2. **Gharana System:** General introduction to different Gharanas of Tabla and Mridang.
3. **Definitions with Examples:** Gat, Sath Paran, Laggi, Ladi, Paran, Delhi Peshkar, Uthan, Paral, Kamali-Chakradar.
4. **Accompaniment Knowledge:** Understanding Talas used for accompanying vocal styles:
   * Dhrupad, Dhamar, Hori, Bada Khayal, Chhota Khayal, Thumri, Bhajan, Tappa, Geet, Ghazal, and Qawwali.
5. **Percussion Overview:** General knowledge of other percussion instruments (*Avanaddha Vadyas*).
6. **Notation:** Writing notation for all prescribed Talas.
7. **Biographies:** Life sketch of any one of the following:
   * Ustad Wajid Hussain Khalifa
   * Pandit Sakharam
   * Pandit Ayodhya Prasad
   * Ustad Masit Khan
   * Ustad Munir Khan

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ MADHYAMA PART - II (THEORY)
**Maximum Marks: 100**

1. Brief history of Indian Music.
2. In-depth study of the historical origin of Tabla and Pakhawaj.
3. Comparative study of different Gharanas and *Baj* (playing styles) of Tabla and Pakhawaj.
4. **The Ten Pranas of Taal (*Taal Ke Das Pran*).**
5. **Carnatic Tala System:** Introduction to the South Indian rhythmic structure.
6. Overview of various North Indian percussion instruments.
7. **Notation Skills:**
   * Writing Thekas of prescribed Talas in *Adi* and *Kuadi* Layakaris.
   * Writing notation for all prescribed compositions.
8. **Biographies:** Life sketch of any one of the following:
   * Kudau Singh
   * Ustad Abid Hussain Khan
   * Nana Saheb Panse
   * Pandit Anokhe Lal Mishra
   * Pandit Samta Prasad (Gudai Maharaj)

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ VISHARAD PART - I (THEORY)
**Maximum Marks: 100**

1. Comparative study of different *Bajs* (playing techniques) of Tabla or Pakhawaj.
2. Advanced analysis of the *Ten Pranas of Taal*.
3. Detailed historical development of Tabla/Pakhawaj.
4. Notation of prescribed Talas in *Adi*, *Kuadi*, and *Biadi* Layakaris.
5. **Definitions and Practical Examples of Technical Terms:**
   * Prakar of prescribed Talas, Rau ($\text{रौ}$), Chala/Chalan, Misil ($\text{मिसिल}$), Bant ($\text{बाँट}$), Pench ($\text{पेँच}$), Angushthana ($\text{अंगुष्ठाना}$), Farad ($\text{फर्द}$), Charbagh ($\text{चारबाग}$), Dupalli ($\text{दुपल्ली}$).
6. **Biographies:** Life sketch of any one of the following:
   * Ustad Ahmed Jan Thirakwa
   * Ustad Habibuddin Khan
   * Pandit Samta Prasad
   * Pandit Ambadas
   * Sri Parbat Singh

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ VISHARAD PART - II (THEORY)
**Maximum Marks: 100**

1. Comprehensive study of the South Indian (Carnatic) Tala system.
2. Distinctive characteristics of various Tabla and Mridang Gharanas and criteria for differentiation.
3. **Definitions and Concepts:**
   * Zarab, Wazen, Ati Vilambit, Ati Drut, Jati (5 types), Yati (5 types), Graha, Tipalli, Chaupalli, Lal Kila, Lom-Vilom, Ekhatthi Paran ($\text{एकहत्थी परन}$), Gat-Qaidas, Gat-Paran, Navhakka ($\text{नवहक्का}$).
4. **Notation Systems:** Comparative study of Pandit Vishnu Narayan Bhatkhande and Pandit Vishnu Digambar Paluskar notation systems.
5. Detailed understanding of North Indian percussion instruments.
6. **Accompaniment Dynamics:** Playing Tabla/Pakhawaj with Vocal Music, Instrumental Music, and Dance.
7. Technical distinctions between **Solo Performance** and **Accompaniment**.
8. **Biographies:** Life sketch of any one of the following:
   * Ustad Abid Hussain
   * Nathan Khan
   * Nana Saheb Panse
   * Kudau Singh
   * Bada Munna Khan

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ NIPUN PART - I (THEORY)

#### Paper I
**Maximum Marks: 100**

1. The Indian philosophy and concept of *Taal*, and the role of percussion instruments in modern times.
2. Comparative study of ancient and modern Tala systems.
3. Critical analysis of different Gharanas and *Baj* (styles) of Tabla or Mridang.
4. **Taal Prastar:** Mathematical calculation and permutation/combination of beats (*Matras*).
5. Theoretical principles governing Solo Performance vs. Accompaniment.
6. In-depth comparative evaluation: North Indian (Hindustani) vs. South Indian (Carnatic) Tala Systems.
7. Influence and application of **Chhand** (meter) in percussion compositions.
8. Contributions and life stories of master exponents of Tabla and Pakhawaj.

#### Paper II: Essay
**Maximum Marks: 100**

An essay of approximately 800 words on any general topic related to music. 

*Sample Topics:*
* Music and Society
* Relationship of Music to other Fine Arts
* Indian Music in the International Arena
* Institutional Music Education vs. Traditional *Guru-Shishya Parampara*

---

### TABLA / PAKHAWAJ NIPUN PART - II (THEORY)

#### Paper I
**Maximum Marks: 100**

* **Revision:** Review of previous advanced theory modules.
1. Comparative analysis of Western drum/percussion instruments and Indian *Taal Vadya*.
2. In-depth study of *Avanaddha Vadyas* as detailed in the historical text **Sangit Ratnakar**.
3. Study of the *Tala Adhyayas* (Rhythm chapters) in ancient Sanskrit treatises.
4. Critical and analytical breakdown of composition terminology with structural examples.
5. Aesthetics and Stage Presence in Indian Classical Music.
6. Interrelationship between **Chhand - Laya - Taal - Rasa**.
7. Detailed classification and examples of different **Yatis**.
8. The operational importance of **Sam** and **Visham Graha** in accompaniment.

#### Paper II: Essay
**Maximum Marks: 100**

An essay of approximately 800 words on a topic of broad musical and cultural interest.

*Sample Topics:*
* Experimentalism and Innovation in Music
* Indian Music and Spirituality
* The Concept and Practice of Fusion (Indian & Western Music)
* The Role of Artists in National Integration
